15>RAG- Retrieval Augmented Generation - Load->Chunks->Embeddings->Store->SimilaritySearch

16>Vector Embeddings- Relationships between words.And perform cosine angles.
    Vectorization: The model encodes each input string as a high-dimensional vector.
    Similarity scoring: Vectors are compared using mathematical metrics to measure how closely related the underlying texts are.

    1>Cosine Similarity 2>Euclidean distnace 3>Dot Product
